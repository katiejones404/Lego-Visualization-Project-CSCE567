Line Graph Notes

This line graph tracks yearly counts of LEGO minifigure head colors from 1989 to the present day. Historically, LEGO used only yellow minifigure heads. Around 2004 - 2006, there is a sharp rise in realistic skin tones, corresponding with LEGO's shift toward licensed themes.

In the 2010s and especially the 2020s, light and medium skin tones increase steadily, while dark tones grow more gradually but consistently. Non-human colors (for example, blue and green) rise as sci-fi and fantasy themes expand.

Key Finding: Diversity in skin tones has increased significantly since the mid-2000s, though lighter tones still dominate.

Pie Chart Notes

This pie chart highlights which LEGO themes contribute the largest number of darker-tone minifigure heads. Star Wars dominates, likely because of its large catalog of characters and sets. Friends, Minecraft, and superhero themes also make major contributions.

Key Finding: Themes with broad world-building and modern franchises drive the most representation of darker skin tones.

Minecraft characters often use darker tones that do not necessarily represent a character's race. These colors can reflect the game style rather than real-world skin tones.

Proportion Chart Notes

This chart can be filtered by Theme Name and Skin Tone Group. Add or remove theme names to compare distributions, and add or remove skin tone groups to compare Non-Human, Yellow, Dark, Medium, and Light categories across themes.

Themes such as Friends, Super Heroes (Marvel/DC), and Star Wars show higher representation of medium and dark skin tones, while older or niche themes often skew toward lighter tones.

Key Finding: Diversity is uneven across themes, with stronger inclusion in several licensed product lines.

Stacked Bar Notes

This visualization compares traditional minifigures with LEGO minidolls (used mainly in Friends and Disney lines). Minidolls overwhelmingly use light skin tones, with medium tones present but rare and dark tones almost nonexistent. Minifigures show a broader range, though light tones still dominate.

Key Finding: Minidolls remain less diverse than minifigures, despite being part of lines often marketed as inclusive.

Line Graph for LEGO Friends Notes

This chart focuses only on LEGO Friends since its launch in 2012. Light skin tones dominate for much of the first decade, while around 2023 there is a clear rise in darker and non-human tones.

That shift aligns with the 2023 Friends reboot, which emphasized diversity and inclusivity.

Key Finding: Friends shows a recent, intentional redesign featuring more diverse skin tones.
